WHIGS LEADER

Sir Robert Walpole

The First Prime Minister (1721–42)

Sir Robert Walpole: Ambitious Whig leader, regarded as the first Prime Minister. His anti-war government was the longest in British history. Promoted trade, reduced land taxes, and received Downing Street!

VS
TORIES OPPOSITION

Henry St John (Lord Bolingbroke)

Tory Leader

Henry St John: Leader of the opposition. Implicated in the 1715 Jacobite rebellion, leading to long-term political exile and Whig supremacy.
